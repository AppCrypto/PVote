\AuthorResponse{9}{\color{blue}We thank the reviewer for requesting a more informative gas analysis. We conducted a supplementary experiment at \((n,l,t)\in\{(10,5,7),(50,10,30),(100,19,59)\}\), with three independent deployments per configuration and separate first and second acceptances (18 transactions). The breakdown reports pairing, scalar multiplication, other precompiles, calldata, storage, memory expansion, and remaining EVM execution. EIP-based prices and traced opcode charges reconstruct all 18 receipt totals with zero gas discrepancy. In particular, the observed \(5n+9l\) scalar multiplications and \(l\) four-term pairing checks agree with the operation analysis. For \((100,19,59)\), memory expansion and other EVM execution account for 70.6\% of steady-state gas, identifying transcript-buffer allocation and dual-RS arithmetic as optimization candidates. The experiment uses an in-memory Geth EVM chain with London gas rules and a permissive local gas limit; no mainnet feasibility or measured optimization speedup is claimed. Raw transactions, receipts, accounting checks, and plots accompany the supplementary results.}
